UNIVERSAL PROMPT — part A (identical for every apartment, never edit)
Attach two images: IMAGE 1 = "the plan" · IMAGE 2 = reference_style.png
================================================================================

THE TWO REFERENCE IMAGES HAVE DIFFERENT ROLES — do not mix them up:

IMAGE 1 is the FLOOR PLAN. It defines the geometry and nothing else: outer contour, wall positions, wall
thickness, room shapes and proportions, window and door positions, and where each piece of furniture
stands. Take the layout only from here.

IMAGE 2 is the TARGET. It is an approved render of a DIFFERENT apartment. Copy from it the camera angle,
the warm light colour palette, the near-black wall caps, the wood floor tone, the warm local lighting,
the white rounded area plates with the gold badge — and above all its LEVEL OF DETAIL AND FINISH. Do NOT
copy its layout, its rooms or its outline. The outline comes only from IMAGE 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Create a clean, precise 3D CGI architectural visualization of a furnished apartment seen from above —
the quality of a professional studio render, matching IMAGE 2 exactly in finish. Ceiling and roof
removed, doll-house cutaway. Camera almost straight overhead with only a slight 5-10 degree tilt so that
wall thickness and a little volume are visible. No wide-angle lens, no fisheye, no strong perspective, no
distortion — the layout must stay as readable as the original floor plan. The apartment entrance door is
at the BOTTOM edge of the image.

STRICT GEOMETRY — the most important rule:
Reproduce the floor plan from IMAGE 1 1:1. Identical outer contour, identical wall positions, identical
wall thickness, identical room proportions and relative sizes, identical position and width of every
window and every door opening. Do not add, remove, merge, move or resize any wall, room, window or door.
Do not invent extra rooms, niches, balconies or corridors. Every room must keep the exact shape and area
shown on the plan.

FURNITURE — read it from the plan:
The furniture is already drawn on IMAGE 1 as 2D symbols. Convert every symbol into its real
three-dimensional object and place it in exactly the same position and orientation as on the plan: beds,
bedside tables, wardrobes, sofas, armchairs, dining tables and chairs, desks, kitchen units, hob, sink,
fridge, bathtub, shower, toilet, washbasin, washing machine, potted plants.
Add no furniture that is not on the plan and omit none that is. No rugs, no paintings, no extra plants.
Furniture must be logically oriented for its room and must not block doors or windows.

MATCH THE DETAIL LEVEL OF IMAGE 2 — this matters as much as the geometry:
IMAGE 2 shows exactly how finished every object must be. Reproduce the same treatment:
- the dining table carries individual place settings, one per chair: a white plate, a knife and a fork
  laid beside it, a small glass;
- the kitchen worktop has a black induction hob set flush into the wood surface, a stainless steel
  kettle, a small ceramic cup or canister, and a white square sink with a slim mixer tap;
- every wardrobe and storage unit is closed with a frosted glass panel lit from inside by a warm LED
  strip; below the glass the garments read as a row of individual hanging shoulders along the rail, and
  the shelves carry neat stacks of folded towels and linen in soft beige, grey and brown tones;
- each bedside table carries a small warm lamp with a visible shade, glowing and casting a pool of light
  on the floor and the wall beside it;
- the bathroom has large beige floor tiles, a white bathtub or shower with a wall mixer and a towel rail,
  a white washbasin with a round mirror, a narrow wall shelf holding a few small bottles and a folded
  towel, and a white wall-hung toilet;
- the washing machine reads from above as a plain flat white rectangle;
- every window has a light sheer white curtain gathered along it;
- the loggia has one large leafy potted plant and a pendant lamp glowing above the armchair.
Every one of these objects is crisp and correctly formed, with clean edges, accurate proportions and a
soft contact shadow. Nothing is blurred, melted, warped, smeared or left as an undefined blob. Keep the
quantity modest — a few well-made objects per room, exactly as in IMAGE 2, never clutter.

PALETTE AND MATERIALS — copy IMAGE 2:
- Light, warm and comfortable colour scheme: warm oak, soft beige, warm white, muted grey.
- Floor: ONE single light warm oak wood-look laminate through the entire apartment — all rooms, kitchen,
  hallway and loggia — same tone, same plank width, same plank direction everywhere.
- The plank pattern runs CONTINUOUSLY from room to room across every doorway — no threshold strip, no
  change of tone or direction, no visible boundary line between rooms.
- Only the bathroom and the WC have a different floor: light beige large-format ceramic tile. Nothing
  else is tiled. The kitchen floor and the hallway floor are the same wood as the rooms — never tiled,
  never stone, never a different shade.
- Walls: light warm white / soft beige plaster, with warm wood wall panelling where IMAGE 2 has it. Wall
  tops (the cut) are a flat matte near-black charcoal cap, exactly as in IMAGE 2 — never light grey.
- All windows are modern white plastic (PVC) windows with white frames.

KITCHEN RULE — base units only:
The kitchen consists ONLY of floor-standing lower cabinets with a continuous worktop, exactly as in
IMAGE 2. There are NO wall cabinets, NO upper cabinets, NO overhead cupboards, NO open wall shelves and
NO shelf units of any kind above the worktop. Seen from above, the worktop, the sink and the hob are
fully visible and the hob is set flush INTO the worktop — never resting on top of a cabinet. The wall
above the worktop is bare.

WASHING MACHINE — critical:
The washing machine stands upright on the floor. Seen from above it is a plain rectangular box with a
flat white top panel facing the camera and nothing on it. Its round glass door is on the vertical front
face, facing sideways into the room, and is therefore NOT visible from above. Never draw a circle, a
porthole, a round door or control dials on the top surface. The machine is never lying on its back.

WARDROBES AND STORAGE:
Every wardrobe, closet and built-in storage unit has a TRANSPARENT frosted glass top so the interior is
fully visible from above, lit by a warm LED strip inside, exactly as in IMAGE 2. Inside, show a small
number of clearly separated items, never a dense undifferentiated mass: a rail with 6-8 garments hanging
HORIZONTALLY along it, seen from above as a neat row of shoulders side by side (never flat against the
wall, never vertical); one or two tidy stacks of folded clothes; a woven basket or a suitcase; 3-4 pairs
of shoes in a row on the bottom shelf. Give the garments soft muted colours that contrast with the light
shelves — warm beige, camel, dusty blue, soft grey, olive, terracotta — never an all-white blur. Every
item reads as a separate object with its own silhouette and a soft shadow.
Wardrobes stay exactly where the plan puts them and never expand into a neighbouring room.

BATHROOM:
The wall segments reserved for plumbing risers and communications must be closed and finished in the same
colour as the wall — no open shafts, no visible pipes, no dark slots.

LIGHTING:
Soft, bright, even daylight plus warm local light sources as in IMAGE 2: bedside lamps, a floor lamp, a
pendant above the dining table, a pendant on the loggia, LED strips inside the wardrobes and under the
kitchen worktop edge. Each lamp glows and throws a soft warm pool of light onto the floor and the nearby
wall. Light, comfortable, airy. No dark or moody interior, no heavy shadows.

CLEAN OUTPUT — remove all 2D drafting symbols:
No hatching, no door swing arcs, no dashed lines, no dimension lines or arrows, no hanger pictograms, no
socket / plug / snowflake / glass icons, no room names, no apartment number, no living-area figure, no
north arrow, no logo, no watermark, no captions, no random text or lettering anywhere in the image.

LABELS — MANDATORY, and the only text allowed in the image:
Every single room must carry its own white area plate — none may be left without one, and none may be
hidden behind furniture. Never omit the plates and never omit the badge.
Small white rounded rectangular plates with dark grey digits, one in the centre of each room, always
upright and horizontal regardless of how the digits are printed on the plan, easy to read on top of floor
and furniture, exactly like the plates in IMAGE 2. The digits are the room areas listed at the end of
this prompt — copy them exactly, invent nothing.
Plus one badge in a free area of the plan: a gold square with the number of rooms and, next to it on a
white field, the total area. No apartment number, no room names, no living area figure.
